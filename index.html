Privacy Policy — MEW (Minimalist Efficient Widget)

Effective date: 16 October 2026
App: MEW — Minimalist Efficient Widget (com.movementoutside.mew)
Developer: MovementOutside (independent developer)
Privacy contact: vibricsolutions@gmail.com

1. Scope and privacy approach

This policy explains how MEW handles information when you use its Android widgets, configuration tools, optional Pro Lifetime purchase and optional advertising or rewarded unlocks. MEW does not require you to create an account with the developer. The app is designed to keep your widget content and settings on your device rather than upload them to a developer-operated cloud account.

Some optional commercial functions depend on Google Play, Google AdMob and a verification service hosted on Cloudflare. Their associated processing is described below. A feature that has not been enabled in a particular version does not generate the corresponding requests solely by being described in this policy.

2. Information stored locally

MEW stores widget and group configurations, shortcuts or actions, user-selected visual resources, preferences, timers and relevant local application state on your Android device. Where a feature needs to inspect installed applications, selected files or Android system capabilities, MEW is designed to use the information locally for that requested feature, without sending your widget content or installed-app inventory to its commercial verification service or advertising providers.

If you choose to export, share, back up or import configurations, the destination or source is under your control. An external backup provider, if chosen by you, may apply its own privacy practices. The developer does not offer a MEW account for synchronizing your widget content.

3. Pro Lifetime purchases and verification

If you purchase or restore Pro Lifetime, Google Play handles checkout and the associated payment information. MEW does not request or store your full payment-card details.

To validate purchase status and prevent fraudulent grants, MEW may exchange with its Cloudflare-hosted verification service an app-specific pseudonymous installation identifier, a Google Play purchase token and its signed purchase evidence, integrity attestation, challenge values and related technical verification metadata. The service may query Google's official Play Developer and Play Integrity APIs. The verification database stores minimal commercial records, such as token hashes, replay-prevention data and verified entitlement status, rather than your widget content. A signed verification result may also be stored securely on your device so previously verified rights can continue when an online check is temporarily unavailable.

Your Google Play account, purchases, payments and refunds are also subject to Google's policies.

4. Optional ads and rewarded unlocks

Some free-version management screens may display ads when enabled. Rewarded ads are optional: you choose whether to watch an available ad to request temporary expanded capacity. Viewing an ad does not by itself grant a reward; MEW checks the ad provider's server-side verification before recording eligible credit.

For reward verification, MEW may process a pseudonymous installation identifier, widget identifier, reward attempt identifier, nonces, validated advertisement transaction identifiers, credit status and expiration information. These commercial identifiers are not intended to contain widget titles, group contents or private actions. Server records also help prevent duplicate rewards and replay attacks.

When Google AdMob's advertising software is enabled and an ad is requested, Google and its advertising partners may process IP address (and approximate location derived from it), device and advertising identifiers where available, ad impressions and interactions (including video viewing), and diagnostic or performance information. Their use may include ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and, where permitted, personalization. MEW does not deliberately send your widget contents to AdMob to target advertising.

Where applicable, MEW uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) and available regional consent or opt-out controls. You may be offered a choice to consent, decline or manage purposes and partners. When a choice or authorization required for an ad is unavailable, the relevant ad or rewarded feature may not be offered; ordinary available functions of MEW remain usable. Pro Lifetime removes MEW's advertising as provided by its product rules.

5. Services and recipients

Depending on the features you use, information may be processed by:

  • Google Play / Google Play Billing / Play Integrity: purchase, restoration, licensing checks and fraud prevention.
  • Google AdMob and its advertising technology partners / Google UMP: consent choices, optional ad delivery, rewarded ads, measurement and fraud prevention. Partner identities and choices are provided through the applicable consent interface.
  • Cloudflare Workers and D1: hosting MEW's commercial verification service and storing the limited verification and anti-abuse records described above. Cloudflare may also process network metadata, including IP address and request timing, to deliver and protect the service.

The developer does not deliberately transmit the contents of widgets, groups, shortcuts, selected images or local settings to these services for advertising. The developer does not independently sell that content. Providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

You can consult Google's Privacy Policy, Google's explanation of advertising data use and Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

6. Purpose and legal grounds

MEW processes only information reasonably needed to provide requested app features, administer verified purchases and rewards, maintain security and prevent abuse, meet legal obligations, and operate advertising where enabled and permitted. Where applicable law requires consent for a particular activity, the relevant consent controls apply. Other processing may be necessary to provide the service requested by you or to protect the service against fraud, subject to applicable law.

Processing by Google and Cloudflare may involve infrastructure or recipients outside your country. Their applicable safeguards and policies govern that processing.

7. Retention and deletion

Widget content and settings primarily remain on your device until you remove them or remove the app. MEW's recoverable-items feature is designed to retain eligible deleted local items for 30 days, unless you permanently delete them earlier. Backups or exports stored outside MEW are not deleted automatically when you remove a widget or uninstall the app.

Commercial verification records are retained only as needed to validate or restore entitlements, reconcile reward credits, prevent duplicated or fraudulent transactions, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. Some transaction and anti-replay records may need to remain after a widget is deleted or the app is uninstalled; uninstalling the app does not automatically delete remote verification records. No fixed universal deletion period is represented for such records while the relevant security and lifecycle controls remain under implementation.

You may contact the developer to request access to, correction or deletion of personal information under our control, where applicable. Because MEW does not create developer accounts, you may need to provide a locally available installation identifier or other proportionate information to identify the relevant pseudonymous records. Do not send purchase tokens, private keys or payment details by email. Some records may be retained where legally required or necessary to prevent fraud, and requests may be limited when ownership cannot be safely verified.

8. Your choices and rights

You can manage local content using MEW's available edit, export and deletion functions, and manage Android permissions through system settings. Where required, MEW will expose options to review or change advertising privacy choices through its UMP interface. Google Play purchases and your advertising identifier can also be managed using Google's and Android's controls.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to information, access, correction, deletion, objection, withdrawal of consent or portability. To make a privacy request, use the contact below. Withdrawing advertising consent does not invalidate a verified Pro purchase or delete local widget configurations.

9. Security

MEW uses Android app storage protections and, where relevant, device-backed security features, cryptographic verification and encrypted HTTPS communications for commercial requests. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We limit the commercial service to the data needed for verification and do not treat local advertising callbacks as authoritative proof of a purchase or reward.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when MEW's functionality, service providers or legal requirements change. The date above will be revised for material updates. The current version will remain accessible from the application's privacy-policy link.
