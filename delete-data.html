Application: MEW — Minimalist Efficient Widget (com.movementoutside.mew)
Developer: MovementOutside

MEW does not require a developer-managed account or login. You can request deletion of personal data held by MovementOutside for MEW, including eligible verification-related records, even if you have uninstalled the app.

How to request data deletion

  1. Send an email to vibricsolutions@gmail.com with the subject MEW — Data deletion request.
  2. State that you wish to delete data associated with MEW. You can identify the data or records concerned, if known. If you no longer have access to MEW, say so.
  3. We may ask for the minimum additional information needed to find relevant pseudonymous records and verify that the request is authorized. We will explain any data we cannot identify or erase and the reason.

Email a deletion request

If the email button does not open your mail app, manually write to vibricsolutions@gmail.com. Do not send passwords, private keys, full payment-card details, or Google Play purchase tokens.

What can be deleted, and what is kept

  • Data on your device: Widget configurations, groups, settings and other functional content are primarily stored locally. You can remove them using MEW's available deletion controls or by deleting the app's data on Android. MEW's recoverable items are designed to remain recoverable for 30 days after being moved to Recently deleted, unless permanently deleted earlier. Uninstalling does not erase any copies you exported to another location.
  • MEW-operated verification records: If present and identifiable, we can review deletion requests concerning pseudonymous installation references, reward-credit records, verification metadata and related records held by the MEW verification service on Cloudflare. Deletion is subject to verifying the request and any necessary security and legal retention.
  • Records retained for security or legal reasons: Minimal transaction verifiers, anti-replay / anti-fraud evidence and records needed to reconcile an outstanding purchase, validated reward or dispute may need to be kept after other data is deleted. They are retained only for the period needed for those purposes or an applicable legal requirement; there is no universal fixed extra retention period for these categories. We will explain the applicable limitation when responding.
  • Data handled by other companies: Google Play manages purchase/payment data, and Google AdMob and its advertising partners may handle advertising data. A request sent to MovementOutside does not automatically erase records independently controlled by these providers. See their privacy controls or contact them for their own records.

What happens after a request

We review which records are within our control, confirm what can be deleted, and explain any retained categories and grounds. MEW does not require users to install the app again to submit a request. Requests are handled within applicable legal time limits.

For more information, see the MEW Privacy Policy.
